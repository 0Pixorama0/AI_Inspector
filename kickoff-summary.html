What we need from you THE ASK

  • Scope sign-off — confirm the 60 features and exclusions; sign the SOW
  • Training data — 1,500–3,000 intake photos + sample devices across your real mix
  • Week-1 workshop — one day to lock defect taxonomy and grading rules
  • Hardware on site by Week 3 — camera rig, lighting, GPU (spec at right)
  • Access — environments, SSO tenant, one ERP/WMS endpoint
  • People — 2 operators + 1 supervisor, ~1 day per sprint (labelling, review, UAT)
  • Decisions — a nominated owner; approvals returned within 2 business days

Acceptance gates PROVEN, NOT PROMISED

  • Model accuracy — recall ≥ 0.90 · precision ≥ 0.85 on the top-10 defect classes, on a frozen holdout set
  • Performance — API p95 < 300 ms at 3× peak; capture-to-grade under 60 s
  • Quality — zero open S1/S2 defects; regression green 3 runs in a row
  • Security & access — third-party pen test clean of high/critical; WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Payment follows milestones — 15 / 15 / 15 / 20 / 15 / 15 % + 5% at warranty end

Capture setup to procure CLIENT-SUPPLIED

  • Booth / fixture — matte enclosure with a repeatable cradle for your device sizes
  • 6 cameras — 12–20 MP industrial (GigE), fixed mounts: top, bottom, left, right, front, keyboard
  • Lighting — diffuse flicker-free LED panels + low-angle raking light for scratches
  • Edge GPU node — one on-site box (RTX-class) running local inference
  • Trigger & ID — single trigger (pedal/button) firing all six; barcode/QR scanner
  • Network & power — PoE gigabit switch; UPS recommended
  • Final spec confirmed in the Week-1 workshop — device mix & throughput set camera and lens choices; we develop against a mock rig so software never waits on hardware
